Variable episodeid (table TSocialgp_head) : EPISODE OF HEADSHIP ID. Type: Identifiant.

Variable headid (table TSocialgp_head) : INDIVIDUAL ID (HEAD ID). Type: Identifiant.

Variable sobserveid (table TSocialgp_head) : Observation at the beginning of the headship. Type: Identifiant.

Variable sdate (table TSocialgp_head) : Date at the beginning of the headship. Type: Date.

Variable eobserveid (table TSocialgp_head) : Observation at the end of the headship. Type: Identifiant.

Variable edate (table TSocialgp_head) : Date at the end of the headship. Type: Date.

Variable eeventtype (table TSocialgp_head) : Reason of ending headship. Type: Catégorielle (codes). Modalites/valeurs codees: 1 or 3 Migrations out 1 or 4 Deaths.
